About

Making worlds feel alive

Jamzey St. is an independent studio making environment tools for Unity: snow that piles up and takes footprints, rain that soaks surfaces, fire that spreads and chars, and surfaces with real depth.

Every package is built and tested in Unity 6, uses its own original textures, meshes and shaders, and is designed to work with the scenes and shaders you already have.
