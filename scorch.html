Scorch

Dynamic Fire & Burn System for HDRP

Mark objects as flammable and they catch, burn, char, smoulder and go out. A glowing front crawls across each surface, neighbours heat up and catch in turn, and water puts it all out again. The char is drawn as a decal, so it works on your existing HDRP materials without changing a single one.

Scorch in motion

Features

What's inside

Burns what you have

  • Char, soot and glowing embers on your existing HDRP materials
  • A glowing burn front that climbs faster than it spreads down
  • Burns move with the object, and burnt stays burnt

Fire that behaves

  • Spreads object to object, faster downwind and upward
  • 7 material presets, from paper and hay to hardwood beams
  • Dies down into smouldering embers before going out

Three flame modes

  • Particles: soft, layered and cheap enough for a burning street
  • Particles Enhanced (1.1): sharp, torn flames baked from a 3D combustion simulation
  • Volumetric: a live 3D simulation for hero fires; switch any time

Water & explosions

  • Hoses, buckets and rain put fires out in a burst of steam
  • Soaked walls make firebreaks that dry out again
  • Explosive objects with chain reactions, or explosions in one call

Smoke, light & sound

  • Smoke by fuel, sparks, embers, ash and heat haze
  • Firelight with soft shadows; soot and blast marks on any surface
  • Original fire, crackle, steam and explosion sounds

Workflow

  • Fire Manager with a live monitor and one-click setup check
  • Quality tiers Low to Ultra with a live cost readout (1.1)
  • C# API with events, heat queries and save/restore
