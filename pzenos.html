Chapter one

The one that started it all

Before the snow, the rain and the fire, there was P_Zenos: my own cinematic third-person action-adventure, built in Unity 6 with HDRP and ray tracing. It's the project Jamzey St. began with.

Every swim, climb, boat ride and explosion on this page is a real-time shot from the game. It's still in development, and you can follow every step of it on Patreon.
