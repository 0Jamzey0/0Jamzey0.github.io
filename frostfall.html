Frostfall

Deformable Snow for HDRP

Open-world snow for the High Definition Render Pipeline. Characters leave boot prints, vehicles carve trenches with pushed-up berms, and every rock, roof and crate collects snow automatically, with no material changes, while the ground under roofs and trees stays bare.

Frostfall in motion

Features

What's inside

Deformable snow ground

  • Tessellated, subsurface-scattered snow with real displacement and shadows
  • GPU deformation simulated by compute shaders, no gaps at speed
  • Berms around every track, compacted floors, trample-to-slush reveal

Snow on any shader

  • One HDRP Custom Pass adds lit snow to every opaque object
  • No material changes: HDRP/Lit, Shader Graph and third-party shaders
  • Sky occlusion keeps roofs, bridges and tree canopies sheltered

Weather & presets

  • Snowfall accumulation, thaw, wetness and track refill
  • Blizzards, wind-driven snow and lens frost (1.1)
  • 5 presets, swappable at runtime

Characters & vehicles

  • One-click footprints for any humanoid, fitted from the rig
  • WheelCollider ruts, hooves, debris and ragdoll contact
  • Contact events plus particle and audio hooks

Workflow

  • Setup Wizard that checks and fixes your project in one click
  • Snow Cover material converts HDRP/Lit in place
  • Live track preview and red/green debug gizmos

Ships with

  • Playable showcase demo with a weather panel, plus a minimal demo
  • Clean C# API, assembly definitions and PDF documentation
  • All textures, meshes and shaders are original
